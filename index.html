The insight. Decision paralysis peaks in the shopping cart, not on the product page. That's where buyers have already committed 3–5 candidates and are silently asking, "which one do I actually keep?" Taobao and JD both answer that question with an in-cart AI assistant. They shipped it in Oct 2025 and have iterated multiple times since. Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop and Temu have not shipped an equivalent. This proposal ports the pattern to Shopee, adapted for SEA buyers.

02 / 04 Problem & precedent

Shopee's cart lists items. It doesn't help buyers choose between them.

Shopee cart today (Sep 2026)

The current cart is a checklist of what's been added. When a buyer has two similar items from different sellers, the interface offers no way to compare them side by side — no spec differences, no price-per-unit, no delivery difference, no clue which voucher applies where. Buyers open two product pages, tab back and forth, and often give up.

The pattern already works — in China

Taobao (千问) and JD (京言) both ship an in-cart AI assistant that lets a buyer select 2+ items, ask "compare these for me," and get a written verdict plus a spec table — without leaving the cart. Launched Oct 2025 and iterated multiple times since. Measurable lifts in checkout initiation and lower cart dwell across every release.

01Trigger lives in the cart. Where the choice is actually being made. Not the PDP.
02Answer first, table second. A one-line verdict up top ("A is cheaper by 5%, B has better reviews"), spec table below for buyers who want detail.
03Handoff to purchase. "Keep this one · remove the other" is one tap — the winner stays in cart, the loser is removed, buyer proceeds to checkout.

04 / 04 Localised for SEA · Impact & rollout

A ported pattern only works when it speaks the local buyer's language.

5 changes vs. the Taobao original
PRICE

Value-per-unit leads, not spec sheets.

SEA buyers optimise for price-per-100ml, not feature counts. The AI's first line is always a value verdict; specs are secondary.

VOUCH

Vouchers are part of the price.

Every price shown is the after-voucher price, with the applicable voucher named inline. Shopee's stacking logic gets translated, not hidden.

LANG

Multilingual, review-grounded.

AI answers in EN / ID / TH / VN / MS. Chinese-language seller descriptions are auto-translated. Every claim cites the review or Q&A it came from.

3G

Verdict streams first, table lazy-loads.

The one-line answer arrives in <600ms; the full spec table loads afterwards. Optimised for 3G / low-end Android common in ID / PH / VN.

TRUST

Every AI answer is reversible.

"Keep the other" undoes any AI-suggested removal in one tap for 24 hours. Trust is earned in retention, not in the first click.
