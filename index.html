Bringing a proven Chinese-market pattern
to Southeast Asia — where nobody has it yet.

The insight. Decision paralysis peaks in the shopping cart, not on the product page. That's where buyers have already committed 3–5 candidates and are silently asking, "which one do I actually keep?" Taobao and JD both answer that question with an in-cart AI assistant. They shipped it in Oct 2025 and have iterated multiple times since. Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop and Temu have not shipped an equivalent. This proposal ports the pattern to Shopee, adapted for SEA buyers.

03 / 04 Solution · 4-step in-cart flow

Four taps from indecision to order — inside the Shopee UI buyers already know.

01A

Entry via top banner

A contextual card appears above cart items when 2+ similar items are detected.

01B

Entry via header shortcut

A subtle "Compare" text appears next to the Edit link. Non-intrusive for buyers who don't need it.

02

Pick the items to compare.

A bottom sheet lists everything in cart. Pre-selects the pair the AI already spotted.

03

Verdict first, table second.

A one-line answer up top; scrollable spec table below; the seller-review sources are one tap away.

04

Full table, one-tap decide.

Every "BEST" tag is derived from real product data. Keep + Buy Now proceeds directly to checkout.

04 / 04 Localised for SEA · Impact & rollout

A ported pattern only works when it speaks the local buyer's language.

5 changes vs. the Taobao original
PRICE

Value-per-unit leads.

AI leads with a value verdict, not a spec sheet.

VOUCH

Voucher-inclusive prices.

Every price is after-voucher, with the specific voucher named.

LANG

Multilingual, grounded.

EN / ID / TH / VN / MS. Every claim cites its source review.

3G

Verdict streams first.

One-line answer in <600ms. Table loads after. Built for 3G.

TRUST

Every action is reversible.

One-tap undo, 24-hour window. Trust before speed.
